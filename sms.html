What you're signing up for

By opting in through the chat widget on this page, you agree to receive text messages from TDR Learning Academy. Messages may include:

  • Enrollment and application reminders
  • School closures, delays, and schedule changes
  • Event, program, and important school announcements
  • Replies to questions you send us (two-way conversation)
Consent is not a condition of enrollment or of any purchase. You don't have to opt in to apply to or attend TDR Learning Academy.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe, or HELP for help. You can also contact us at (407) 694‑8595 or Hello@tdracademy.org.

Privacy

We respect your privacy. The mobile phone number and information you provide when you opt in are used only to send you the messages described above and to respond to you. We do not sell or share your mobile opt-in information or phone number with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties for marketing. Information may be shared only with service providers that help us operate our messaging program (for example, our messaging platform), and only as needed to deliver the service. Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Terms

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. For help, reply HELP to any message or email Hello@tdracademy.org. To stop receiving messages, reply STOP; you will receive a single confirmation and no further messages unless you opt in again.
