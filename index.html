About Us

Educating the whole child —
mind, body, spirit.

Our mission is to educate the whole child. We believe every student can learn and achieve at high levels, and we are committed to providing the resources and support they need to succeed. Education at TDR is more than academics — it is about developing well-rounded individuals prepared to make a positive impact on the world.

We believe learning is most effective when it is personalized, engaging, and challenging. All students have unique learning styles, and we are committed to providing instruction and support that prepares them for success in college, career, and life — rooted in a Christian worldview.

Curriculum

Four pillars.
One student.

Our curriculum integrates Biblical teaching throughout every subject area, developing the whole child academically, physically, and spiritually.

📐

Common Core

Standards-aligned instruction in Mathematics, English Language Arts, Science, and Social Studies — including ESL support. Students cover subjects from foundational arithmetic through Algebra, Biology, American History, and more.

✝️

Bible Studies

Scripture is not an add-on at TDR — it runs through everything we do. Students study the life and teachings of Jesus, Old and New Testament, and develop a personal relationship with their faith at every grade level.

🏃

Physical Education

Physical instruction through routine exercise, team sports, Health, and Life Skills. PE builds discipline, teamwork, and healthy habits that serve students well beyond the classroom and into adult life.

🎨

Electives

Art, Foreign Language, Music, and more. Electives give students space to discover and develop their individual gifts — celebrating what makes each child uniquely capable of something extraordinary.

Admissions

Start your
new journey.

We welcome applications year-round. The best time to apply is mid-April through end of June. Our office closes in July — apply early to secure your seat. Currently enrolling new students across all grades K–12.

1

Submit Your Interest

Fill out our quick enrollment form with your contact info and your child's name and grade. We'll respond with the full application packet and next steps.

2

Send Your Documents

Email your completed application to Hello@tdracademy.org or bring it in person. Includes immunization records, birth certificate, report card, and a few other standard documents.

3

Pay the Application Fee

$125 for a new student, with a $25 sibling discount for each additional child ($100 for the second, $75 for the third) — or $50 for re-enrollment. Payable over the phone or in person. Once received, your application is officially in review.

Scholarships

Could your child attend
tuition-free?

Since 2023, Florida's school-choice scholarships are open to every K–12 student in the state — regardless of household income. Most TDR families use the Family Empowerment Scholarship (FES-EO) or the Florida Tax Credit Scholarship (FTC) to cover most or all of their tuition.

Answer three quick questions to see where your family stands. Nothing you click is saved or sent anywhere — and our office is happy to walk you through the real application at no obligation.

Learn more at FLDOE School Choice or call us at (407) 694-8595.
